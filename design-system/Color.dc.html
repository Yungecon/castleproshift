Foundations 03

Color

Three families and nothing else: one clay, a black held at four values, and the paper. Every grey in the system is black at an opacity, so nothing carries a warm or cool cast that fights the clay.

Clay
Clay #b7674f
oklch(59.9% .109 37.5) Rules, eyebrows, the pipe between prices, gutter markers.
Clay deep #9a5239
oklch(52.0% .103 39.7) Text only. The editorial aside and link colour, where clay would sit too light on paper.
Clay tint #f3e7e0
oklch(93.6% .016 52.6) Held in reserve. No shipped page fills with it yet — see the open question below.
Ink, paper, rule
Ink #000000
100% Titles, producers, labels, the hard rule above a definition list.
Ink soft rgba(0,0,0,.78)
78% Running prose. Every paragraph in the system sets here, never at full black.
Mute rgba(0,0,0,.45)
45% Third rank of the entry line — origin and vintage — and the folio.
Paper #ffffff
chroma 0 The sheet. Pure, so the clay is the only warmth on the page.
Line rgba(0,0,0,.14)
14% Dividers inside a definition list, and the two folio hairlines.
Line hard #000000
1px Opens a definition list. One per block, at the top edge only.
How clay behaves
Rule Clay draws lines and sets small caps. It is not a fill, a highlight, a button or a background band.
Budget Per page: the eyebrow, the section hairlines, the price pipe, the gutter markers, and at most one clay italic aside. Past that it stops reading as an accent.
Extending Both clays hold chroma near .105 at hue 38 and differ only in lightness. A new step moves L and nothing else — that is what keeps the ramp from drifting orange.
Grayscale Clay at 59.9% lightness and mute black at 45% converge when the page is photocopied. Never let clay be the only thing separating two ranks of information.
Banned No gradients, anywhere, including the footer rule the memo set used to carry. No second accent hue. No warm greys.
Open Clay tint is defined but unused. It stays a declared token with no permitted use until a page needs one — deleting it loses the value, using it casually breaks the fill ban.
