Foundations 05

Space

The sheet is Letter authored at 96 pixels to the inch, so a pixel here is a real measurement and 12pt body type is 16px. Everything is separated by space; almost nothing is separated by a line.

The sheet
672
measure
Sheet 816 × 1056. Letter at 96ppi. A4 is 794 × 1123, A5 559 × 794, Tabloid 1056 × 1632.
Margin 72 on all four sides. Not a grid unit — a physical three-quarter inch, and the reason the page reads as a printed sheet.
Measure 672 for lists, which use the full column. Prose caps at 600 regardless, or the 14.5px body runs past a comfortable line.
Two column Equal halves with a 48 gutter. Never three on a Letter sheet at this margin.
Foot The folio is pushed down by auto margin, never positioned. Short pages keep their foot at the bottom edge.
The step scale
8Inside a price pair, either side of the clay pipe (9).
12Eyebrow to title. Definition-row padding, top and bottom.
16Paragraph spacing. Entry body to price. Tag column to its definition.
24Section label to its hairline. Accent rule to the paragraph under it.
32Between stacked editorial blocks inside one column.
48Above a section head. Column gutter. Content to the folio rule.
64Reserved. Available for a full-sheet break; unused so far.
Rhythm and reflow
Asymmetric A section head takes 48 above and 16 below. The head belongs to what follows it, and the space says so.
Tight lists Entries take 3 above and below — a 6px channel. It is deliberately below the step scale: entries are one block, not seven objects.
Gap, not margin Groups of siblings are laid out with flex or grid and a gap. Per-element margins and whitespace between inline elements do not survive editing.
Below 900 The sheet gives up its fixed width and its margin drops to 40 / 24. Two columns become one at a 24 gap. Type sizes do not change.
On screen Sheets sit on black at 6% with a 40 gap between them, so a stack of pages reads as paper rather than as a scrolling document.
