Editorial
Mono

Design system · v1

One typeface, one accent, two registers. Everything below was read back out of documents that already exist and already agree with each other — a 44-page list and a memo set — rather than invented to sit above them.

Where the two disagreed, the disagreement was kept and scoped by document type instead of averaged away. That is the whole premise: a system with two correct answers, and a rule for which one applies.

The split
Memo register

Display carries the weight. 700 headings, 400 body, a 46×3 clay bar under the heading. For anything argued in prose.

List register

Weight inverts. 400 titles at scale, 700 only at line level on the producer, a clay hairline that measures the column. For anything enumerated.
