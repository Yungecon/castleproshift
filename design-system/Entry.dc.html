The entry

One line, three ranks, no rule. It carries every item on 44 pages and it never changes shape — only which of its optional slots are present. Read left to right the line falls from most to least load-bearing, and it does that with weight, slope and value instead of size.

Rules of the line
Baseline Every slot aligns on the baseline, not the box. Sizes may differ across slots without the line stepping.
No leaders No dotted or dashed run between the name and the price, on any of 44 pages. The right-aligned tabular column does the work and the eye crosses the white space unaided.
No dividers Nothing between entries but the 6px channel. Rules belong to section heads only.
Wrapping The body may wrap to a second line; the value never leaves the first. That is what the min-width on the body box is protecting.
Currency No symbol, anywhere. The column is understood, and a repeated glyph would compete with the clay pipe.
