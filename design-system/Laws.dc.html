Components 09

Laws

What the system will not do, what it has settled, and what it has not decided yet. An item stays on this page until it moves category; nothing is removed quietly.

Never
Gradients Nowhere, at any opacity. The memo footer gradient was translated into two flat hairlines and will not come back.
Leaders No dots or dashes running to a price. Confirmed across all 44 pages.
Row rules No divider between entries. Rules appear at section heads, at the top edge of a definition list, and at the foot. Three places, no others.
Warm grey Greys are black at an opacity. A tinted grey next to clay reads as a printing fault.
Clay fills Clay is a line and a small-caps colour. No filled bands, tinted rows, buttons, chips or left-border callouts.
Second face No serif, no display face, no mono outside a token listing. Range comes from tracking, case and value.
Mixed register One register per sheet. A page that wants both is two pages.
Ornament No emoji, dingbats, icons, rounded corners, shadows or hairlines under 1px. Nothing on the page that would not survive being printed.
Settled
Weight Memo puts bold on top; list puts bold inside the line. Scoped by document type, never averaged.
Italic Black italic is a structural slot. Clay italic is a voice, one per page. A page never uses both meanings.
Tracking Four rungs, each claimed by one role. A new small-caps element joins a rung; it does not invent a value between two.
Figures Every number in the system is tabular and right ranged. No currency symbols.
Prose Body sets at 78% black, not full black, and caps at a 600px measure.
Open
Markers The N and G glyphs sit in the gutter at 700 clay — the only place clay carries a letterform rather than a rule. Keep them clay, or move them to mute black and leave clay purely linear. Unresolved.
Clay tint Declared, unused, and currently illegal under the fill ban. Either find it a legal job or retire the token.
Cover step The 54px cover title and the 26px sub-heading on these boards are additions, not readings. Both hold their register, but no shipped document has used either.
Body floor List entries set at 13.5px, which is under a 12pt print floor at 96ppi. Defensible for scanned lines; it would not be for a page of prose. Decide before the register is used for anything read at length.
Bold italic The fourth cut is loaded and has no assigned role. First use should be a decision, not an accident.

To extend the system, add to the ladder or the step scale before adding a value between two existing ones, and put the new item on this page as open until a real document has used it twice. A system with two correct answers only survives if every third answer has to argue for itself.
