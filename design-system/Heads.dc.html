Why the hairline, not the bar

A 46 × 3 bar is a fixed mark: it announces a section and stops. On a list page, where the reader is about to scan a column and land on a right-aligned figure, the head has a second job — it has to show how wide that column is.

The inline label plus hairline does both. It marks the start and it measures the measure, which is why the list register gave up the bar and the memo register kept it. Neither mark appears on the other register's pages.
